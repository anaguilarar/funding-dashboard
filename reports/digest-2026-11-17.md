### ArcX Climate Change Resilience - Formation of a Network of Regional Centres of Excellence on Climate Change Resilience in Sub-Saharan Africa (Call 2026_ArcX_G261)
*21 days to deadline*

- **Funder:** European Centre for Medium-Range Weather Forecasts (ECMWF), on behalf of the European Union (DG INTPA, NDICI-Global Europe), with EC JRC — EU Africa Regional Centres of Excellence (ArcX) Programme - Climate Change Resilience component (Global Gateway)
- **Official link:** https://www.ecmwf.int/en/about/grants/arcx-grants
- **Status:** open (official) · **Deadline:** 2026-12-09 14:00 CET (21 days left)
- **Funding:** A total budget of EUR 1,500,000 is available for this Call with the aim to award six Grants. Tier 1-2: EUR 250,000-350,000; Tier 3: EUR 150,000-200,000; Tier 4: EUR 50,000. Lump sum grant; budget categories incl. personnel costs; 25% flat-rate indirect costs.
- **Eligibility:** Potentially eligible — clarify. International organisations are explicitly eligible as participants (Schedule 1 B), so the Alliance (Bioversity International, Italy = EU Member State; Nairobi office) could join a multi-beneficiary grant as a Beneficiary (with budget) or Associated Partner (no budget). The Alliance cannot realistically be Coordinator, because the Coordinator must be the prospective RCoE-C2R with a formal regional mandate recognised by at least 2 African governments or an IGO (e.g., ICPAC, AGRHYMET, SADC CSC). Best route: co-beneficiary with a regional climate centre, bringing agricultural climate-services, EO/AI and crop-impact expertise.
- **Consortium required:** optional
- **Fit:** 69/100 (medium) — AI-enabled climate services, EO/Copernicus data use and agriculture-sector climate information are central; strong match to EO/AI/climate-data profile, though the grant mainly funds centre operations and networking rather than research.
- **Themes:** Climate adaptation, Climate data infrastructure, AI / machine learning, Remote sensing & EO, Food security, Capacity building
- **Next action:** By 15 Nov 2026: identify a mandated RCoE-C2R candidate (ICPAC is the natural fit for the Nairobi office), agree role as Beneficiary and budget share; submit clarification questions before 25 Nov 2026.
