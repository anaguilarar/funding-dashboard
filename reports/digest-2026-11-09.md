### MSCA Doctoral Networks 2026 - HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01 (Bioversity International as beneficiary/recruiting partner)
*14 days to deadline*

- **Funder:** European Commission (Horizon Europe) — Horizon Europe - Marie Sklodowska-Curie Actions, Doctoral Networks (incl. Joint and Industrial Doctorates)
- **Official link:** https://ec.europa.eu/info/funding-tenders/opportunities/portal/screen/opportunities/topic-details/HORIZON-MSCA-2026-DN-01-01
- **Status:** open (official) · **Deadline:** 2026-11-24 17:00 CET/CEST (Brussels local time, 17:00) (14 days left)
- **Funding:** Call budget EUR 628.03 million. Unit contributions per doctoral candidate per person-month: living allowance EUR 4,250 (x country correction coefficient; Italy 93.8%), mobility allowance EUR 710, family allowance EUR 660 (if applicable); institutional: research, training and networking EUR 1,600 and management and indirect EUR 1,200. EU contribution capped at 540 person-months per network.
- **Eligibility:** Eligible (confirmed). Bioversity International (international organisation headquartered in Italy) is deemed established in Italy for MSCA DN and, as an IO HQ'd in a Member State, is eligible for funding in 'Training and mobility' actions (General Annex B). It can therefore be a funded beneficiary that recruits and hosts doctoral candidates (and could count as one of the three MS/AC entities). It is not a degree-awarding body, so a partner university must enrol the candidates. CIAT (IO HQ in Colombia) is not covered by the IO clause and would participate as an associated partner hosting secondments in Colombia/Kenya/etc. Max 40% of EU contribution to one IO. Mobility rule for candidates recruited by an IO: not more than 12 of the last 36 months in the same appointing organisation.
- **Consortium required:** yes
- **Fit:** 64/100 (medium) — Bottom-up: a network on EO/AI for climate-resilient agriculture or crop modelling would fit; but it funds doctoral candidates, not the researcher's own salary.
- **Themes:** Capacity building, Climate adaptation, Digital agriculture, Remote sensing & EO, AI / machine learning, Crop modelling, Biodiversity
- **Next action:** Contact Bioversity grants office to confirm PIC status/LEAR and willingness to recruit doctoral candidates; post a partner-search request on the portal for DN 2026/2027 networks on EO/AI for agriculture.
- **Last verified:** 2026-10-22
